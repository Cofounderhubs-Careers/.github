## About

CofounderHubs is a single platform where startups and entrepreneurs discover co-founders, candidates, investors, and business opportunities.

We combine networking, co-founder discovery, talent and recruitment, job posting, and investor connections — with optional recruitment support for companies that want hands-on help.

## Mission

**Simplify the startup ecosystem.**

Bring founders, co-founders, candidates, investors, and companies together in one place, reducing the time and difficulty of finding the right people and opportunities — so startups can focus on building and growing.

In practice, that means helping teams:

- Find the right co-founders and early team members
- Recruit qualified candidates efficiently
- Promote job opportunities to a startup-focused audience
- Discover potential investors and business connections
- Access recruitment support when they need it

## Vision

**A trusted global startup hub.**

To become a trusted global platform where startups, entrepreneurs, talented professionals, co-founders, and investors connect and build meaningful business opportunities together — making the search for people, opportunities, and resources faster, easier, and more accessible.

## Core Values

| Value | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| **Innovation** | Continuously improving how startups discover co-founders, talent, investors, and opportunities — through clearer profiles, better search, and practical hiring tools. |
| **Global Access** | Great teams and partnerships form across cities, countries, and time zones. Location should never block the right connection. |
| **Collaboration** | Building trust between founders, candidates, and companies through profiles, feed conversations, job posts, and real connections. |
| **Trust** | Transparent profiles, honest matching, and respect for privacy in every connection and conversation. |

## What the Platform Does

- **Co-founder Discovery** — Match with potential co-founders by skills, interests, and goals
- **Talent & Recruitment** — Source and evaluate candidates built for startup teams
- **Job Posting** — Publish roles and reach startup-minded professionals
- **Investor Connections** — Discover and connect with relevant investors
- **Community Feed** — Share updates, ideas, and opportunities with the ecosystem
- **Recruitment Support** — Optional partner-assisted hiring for companies
- **MVP Development** — Support for turning an idea into a working product
- **HireProof** — Hiring assessment application
